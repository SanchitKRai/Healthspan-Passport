## 3. Safety, Human-in-the-Loop & Failure Mode Analysis

1. **Unflagged Error Protection:**
   Values parsed below 80% confidence or outside `PHYSIOLOGICAL_LIMITS` are marked `needs_review: true`, requiring user confirmation in the UI before PhenoAge is computed. Even in offline mode, `02_metropolis_prediabetic.pdf` produced 1 unflagged mismatch, underscoring why human confirmation remains mandatory.
2. **Offline vs. LLM Extraction:**
   The metrics above reflect **offline heuristic mode** (`HEURISTIC_PATTERNS` + `RapidOCR`). Phone camera photos achieve **0.0%** offline under regex matching. Configured OpenRouter slugs in `backend/app/llm/client.py` are `nvidia/nemotron-3.5-lightning` (primary) and `moonshotai/kimi-k2` (fallback), which have not yet been benchmarked live in this repo.
3. **Missing Biomarker Integrity:**
   When essential tests (such as hs-CRP) are absent, the PhenoAge engine returns `phenoage: null` and lists the missing markers rather than imputing them.

## 4. PhenoAge Validation Status

- **(a) Formula Implementation:** `backend/app/core/phenoage.py` uses the published Levine et al. (2018) coefficients and is unit-tested via `pytest`. A synthetic dataset (`data/synthetic_cohort_unit_test.csv`) generated by `backend/scripts/validate_nhanes.py` is used **strictly for unit-testing the scoring and regression code paths**; because its mortality outcomes are simulated from the Levine coefficients themselves, metrics on it are circular and are **not** reported as validation.
- **(b) Real-NHANES Replication Status:** **In progress (Task 3).** Public-use NHANES laboratory/examination datasets and public-use NCHS linked mortality files exist and will be used for independent replication in Task 3.

## 5. Indian Population Transferability & Limitations

- **Cohort Calibration:** The published Levine PhenoAge algorithm was trained on the United States NHANES population. Baseline reference intervals and cardiometabolic risk thresholds differ in Indian populations.
- **Indian Clinical Customization:** Healthspan Passport normalizes extracted laboratory values against Indian reference thresholds (e.g., Fasting Glucose < 100 mg/dL, HbA1c < 5.7%, Total Cholesterol < 200 mg/dL, Triglycerides < 150 mg/dL).
- **Regulatory Framing:** Biological age is presented solely as an educational healthspan metric and pre-consultation aid, not a medical diagnosis.
